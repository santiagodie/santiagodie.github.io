Scope: src/index.html, rendered to /, /es/ and /fr/ by tools/build.py. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads; the interface recedes).

Audience and job: technical recruiters and engineering teams (Spain + English-speaking countries) screening Santiago in minutes, often from LinkedIn on a phone. Action: open a demo or repo, then reach out via GitHub, LinkedIn or CV.

Constraints: brief-pinned v2 world (DESIGN.md non-negotiables: white page, bone alternating sections, navy text/blocks, gold only on navy or as thin lines on light, unmodified SD logo navy/gold, no flashy effects, reduced motion). Static HTML per language, no content injected by JS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio reads like the owner's own model output: the name, then the headline as tokenizer chips and a next-token prediction, then a gold pipeline thread that carries the visitor through numbered stages to real project evidence. It refuses the dark developer template and the generic hero-plus-card-grid.
OWN-WORLD: White and bone grounds, navy type and blocks, gold reduced to 1-1.5px frames, rules, nodes and bars. Hubot Sans wide and heavy for display, Mona Sans for reading, JetBrains Mono for every datum (ids, numbers, metrics, periods, codes).
STORY: Who (name), what (tokenized headline), proof (featured demos with README metrics), path (experience, education, skills), contact on a navy close.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky transparent header (navy logo, links, square social buttons, EN/ES/FR menu). Hero: name at display scale left, SD monogram drawing itself right (above the name on phones); below, tokenize() chips left and predict_next() bars right of a gold hairline; primary navy button and underlined link.
FORM: Editorial numbered sections on a 12-column grid, brief-pinned; seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the monogram draws once as one stroke; the gold thread draws with scroll (CSS view timelines) through each section node.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
